Privacy Policy

प्रशासनिक शब्दावली (Prashasnik Shabdavali) · Administrative Hindi, 6 words a day

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

प्रशासनिक शब्दावली (“the app”) is developed and published by V C Srivastava, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). The app is available on Google Play (package name prashasanik.shabdavali), as a directly downloadable Android app, and as a website at vcsrivas.github.io/prashasnik-shabdavali.

Contact: vcsrivas.app.dev@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.
  • There is no sign-up, no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The Android app asks for no permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • Your word history is saved only on your own device.

3. Information stored on your device

To show six new words each day and to avoid repeating words, the app keeps a day-wise list of the words it has shown you (the date and the words). This list is saved in the app’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to me or to anyone else.

  • You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s storage (Android: Settings → Apps → प्रशासनिक शब्दावली → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app. On the website, clear the site’s data in your browser.
  • If you have turned on Android’s own backup to your Google account, Android may include this word history in that backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is controlled by you and by Google, not by me.

4. Information I collect

None. The app has no server of its own and does not send any information about you or your use of the app to me.

If you choose to email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you and do not share it.

5. Internet use and third-party services

  • Android app: the word list and all app files are built into the app, and the app works fully offline. It does not request internet access.
  • Website version: the website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads a font from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive technical information needed to deliver the page (such as your IP address and browser type) under their own privacy policies: GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy. I do not receive this information.
  • “सुनें” (Listen) button: this uses the text-to-speech engine already on your phone or computer to pronounce the word. Only the Hindi word being read out is passed to that engine.
  • Links: links to other websites (for example, the download link on the website) open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • Google Play: when you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information under the Google Privacy Policy. Any statistics I see in the Google Play Console (such as the number of installs or crash reports) are anonymous and provided by Google.

6. Children

The app is a vocabulary learning tool suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

As no personal information leaves your device, there is no data held by me that could be exposed. The app is distributed through Google Play and from the project’s own GitHub page.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling information differently, I will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

For any question about this policy or the app, write to V C Srivastava at vcsrivas.app.dev@gmail.com.
